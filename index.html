Every great program
needs a good mentor.

In four semesters of loops, logic and late-night lab submissions, our teachers have been the compilers that turned our confusion into something that actually runs. They've patiently traced our errors — in code, and otherwise.

This Teachers' Day, the 2nd Year CSE batch is pausing the clock cycle to say thank you — with a celebration built the way we build everything else: with a little chaos, a lot of heart, and zero fatal errors.

How the day unfolds

09:30 AM

Registration & Welcome

Faculty and students gather in the seminar hall as the CSE 2nd year committee kicks off the celebration.

Welcome Address

An opening note from the student committee, followed by a formal welcome to all the teachers of the department.

10:45 AM

Cultural Performances

Music, dance and short skits performed by students, dedicated to the faculty of the department.

Light-hearted games and interactive rounds bringing teachers and students together outside the classroom.
